# Third-party assets

- **Assistant**: locally bundled using `@fontsource/assistant`; SIL Open Font
  License 1.1. Source: https://fontsource.org/fonts/assistant . The package includes
  the font license at `node_modules/@fontsource/assistant/LICENSE`.
- **Lucide**: SVG interface icons from `lucide-react`; ISC license. Source:
  https://lucide.dev . See the installed package's `LICENSE` file.
- **Simple Icons 16.14.0**: Apple, Meta, and Shopify SVG paths in `public/logos/`,
  downloaded from the versioned npm package through jsDelivr. Original source:
  https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons . CC0 license copied to
  `public/logos/SIMPLE-ICONS-LICENSE.md`. Only SVG fill colors were changed.

Company names and logos identify illustrative instruments in this assignment.
Other circular marks are local approximations or text fallbacks. The platform
wordmark and triangular mark are a local visual approximation of the recording.
